Summary

Backend developer with 7 years of commercial ecommerce experience (2019–2026), all at a specialist ecommerce agency. Built and shipped complete online stores from the ground up — architecture, custom modules, ERP and payment integrations, data migration, deployment and production maintenance — and owned client relationships end to end. Worked across PrestaShop, WooCommerce, Sylius and a microservice-based B2B marketplace (Lumen, MongoDB, Docker, React), on PHP 5.6 through 8.4 with Symfony, MySQL and PHPUnit.

Contributed to official PrestaShop payment modules for Kustom, Klarna and Mollie. Comfortable leading delivery with junior developers, running code reviews, automating repetitive engineering work and handling security audits. Expanding into Go as a second backend language, building Go services alongside the PHP work.
